## Description

This simple Ceremony is taught to all priests before they are released to wander the wider world. The priests of Shalim share a bond with Apolleon, the Traveler, and can use his presence in eternal Oblivion as a connection between themselves and their followers.

## Requirements

Prerequisite power: Oblivion’s Sight (Chicago by Night).

## Ingredients

The black book received during indoctrination into the cult.

## Process

Holding their black book and thinking of another Shalimite's name, a priest sends a wave through Oblivion to call that individual to their location. This does not compel the target to travel. It alerts the target to the caller's current location through repeated, intermittent visions of the surroundings.

## System

The cultist must possess a black book and know the name of another Shalimite. The vampire's player makes a Ceremony roll at Difficulty 3. The target vampire may ignore the call, but the flashing vision imposes −2 dice on all tests involving concentration for the rest of the scene, after which the call disappears. A critical win allows the caller to send a one-word message along with the vision.
