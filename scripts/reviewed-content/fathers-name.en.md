## Description

Shalim's priests are trained to use their voices as weapons, stripping away the sweetness with which their victims surround their love for the world. Invoking their dark master's name and asking for his help, they channel a fraction of his power against an adversary, clouding the victim's mind with shadows and leaving them stunned by the emptiness of Oblivion.

## Requirements

Prerequisite power: Shadow Perspective (Chicago by Night).

## Ingredients

The ability to speak ancient Greek, eye contact with the victim, and five sticks of charcoal.

## Process

The priest chants in a dialect of ancient Greek, invoking Shalim's name while breaking four charcoal sticks with their hands. They speak while making eye contact with the victim; the victim must be able to see and hear the user for the power to succeed. When the last piece of charcoal breaks, a shadow crosses both the priest's and the victim's eyes, turning them completely black. The victim succumbs to overwhelming despair. Survivors describe an all-consuming darkness closing around their thoughts and washing away every sensation. Their last memory is distant, booming laughter echoing in their mind.

## System

The vampire's player makes a Ceremony roll against the victim's Resolve + Composure. A win allows the vampire to activate the Ceremony's effects while in the victim's presence. After activation, the victim is paralyzed by despair for a number of turns equal to the margin of success. The victim cannot see, hear, or experience any sensory reaction except touch and physical pain, which end the effect. The victim can spend Willpower equal to the number of turns they would remain paralyzed to free themselves.
