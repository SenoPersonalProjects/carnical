## Description

Only Shalim's most powerful priests have manifested this power. Its effect is among the most terrifying and impressive uses of Oblivion witnessed in modern nights. The ability to cast an enemy into Oblivion terrifies even the toughest Kindred.

## Requirements

Prerequisite power: Tenebrous Avatar (Chicago by Night).

## Ingredients

A pot of ink, at least three liters of blood from an innocent murdered by the user, and a dark room. The power does not work outdoors.

## Process

The vampire personally kills an innocent mortal, probably incurring Stains according to the chronicle Tenets and their Convictions. Innocence is subjective; traditional sacrifices include children, virgins, and saints. The vampire brings at least three liters of the deceased's blood into a dark room and paints a door on the wall with it. They throw a pot of ink onto the blood-painted portal. Concentrating their will on that passage, the priest opens a rift into Oblivion.

Anyone falling into the rift is immediately transported into a pocket of eternal black nothingness for as long as the priest sees fit. If the priest is destroyed without releasing their prisoners, all undead prisoners remain trapped in the void unless and until another vampire reverses the Ceremony.

Priests may pass through the portal themselves, accepting eternal damnation. Some Shalimites do this when they feel they have completed their work for the cult.

## System

After following the ceremonial steps, the priest's player makes the Ceremony roll. On a success, the effect is swift and implosive. A hole opens where ink and blood mix, drawing in nearby objects, air, and individuals. Individuals failing a Dexterity + Athletics test at Difficulty 4 are sucked into a pocket of Oblivion.

Prisoners remain suspended in endless darkness, unable to see or hear anything or anyone. Until the priest is destroyed, only that priest can release them. This requires pouring vampire vitae onto the painted door, in a quantity sufficient to cause a Rouse Check. Mortals drawn into Oblivion die instantly.
